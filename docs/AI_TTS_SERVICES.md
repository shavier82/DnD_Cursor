# AI Text-to-Speech Services Guide

This guide covers the best AI-quality TTS services for natural-sounding D&D narration.

## Top AI TTS Services (2025)

### 1. **ElevenLabs** ⭐ Premium Quality
**Best for:** Highest quality, most natural voices

**Features:**
- Extremely natural, human-like voices
- Voice cloning from samples
- 29 languages
- Emotional expression control
- Very low latency

**Pricing:**
- Free: 10,000 characters/month
- Starter: $5/month - 30,000 characters
- Creator: $22/month - 100,000 characters
- Pro: $99/month - 500,000 characters

**API:** Yes, well-documented
**Quality:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Best)

---

### 2. **Play.ht** ⭐ Best Value
**Best for:** Balance of quality and affordability

**Features:**
- 900+ voices across 140 languages
- Voice cloning available
- SSML support
- Good quality neural voices

**Pricing:**
- Free: 2,500 words/month
- Creator: $15/month - 250,000 words
- Pro: $39/month - 600,000 words

**API:** Yes
**Quality:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good)

---

### 3. **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech**
**Best for:** Enterprise, high volume

**Features:**
- 220+ voices in 40+ languages
- WaveNet voices (very natural)
- Neural2 voices (latest, best quality)
- SSML support
- Good free tier

**Pricing:**
- Free: 0-4 million characters/month (first 4M free)
- Paid: $4 per 1M characters (after free tier)

**API:** Yes, well-integrated
**Quality:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good)

---

### 4. **Amazon Polly**
**Best for:** AWS integration

**Features:**
- 100+ voices in 41 languages
- Neural TTS voices
- SSML support
- Good quality

**Pricing:**
- Free: 5M characters/month (first 12 months)
- Paid: $4 per 1M characters (standard), $16 per 1M (neural)

**API:** Yes (AWS SDK)
**Quality:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good)

---

### 5. **Azure Cognitive Services TTS**
**Best for:** Microsoft ecosystem

**Features:**
- Neural voices (very natural)
- 400+ voices in 140+ languages
- SSML support
- Good free tier

**Pricing:**
- Free: 500,000 characters/month
- Paid: $15 per 1M characters

**API:** Yes
**Quality:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good)

---

### 6. **Lovo AI**
**Best for:** Creative projects, voice cloning

**Features:**
- 500+ voices in 100+ languages
- Voice cloning
- Emotional expression
- Good quality

**Pricing:**
- Free: Limited
- Paid: $19/month+

**API:** Yes
**Quality:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good)
