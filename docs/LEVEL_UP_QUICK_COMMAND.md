## Key Level Milestones

### Level 3
- **All Classes:** Subclass choice (Primal Path, Martial Archetype, Divine Domain, etc.)

### Level 4
- **All Classes:** First Ability Score Improvement

### Level 5
- **Major Power Spike!**
- **Martial Classes:** Extra Attack
- **Casters:** 3rd-level spells
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2 → +3

### Level 6
- **Barbarian:** Path Feature, Rage (4/day)
- **Fighter:** Extra ASI
- **Cleric:** Channel Divinity (2/rest)
